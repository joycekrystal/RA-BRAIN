---
name: design-review
description: "Use when reviewing Figma designs, reconciling designs with acceptance criteria, or preparing a design handoff or new design brief."
---

Read ../delivery-standards/references/shared-rules.md. Retrieve the specified Figma file/node and available design-system information alongside the feature specification and Jira criteria. Check primary and alternate flows, loading/empty/error/success states, responsive behavior, keyboard/focus behavior, labels and semantics, permissions, destructive actions and reusable components. Tie findings to specific source nodes and criteria. Distinguish observed defects from proposed refinements and unavailable evidence. Deliver prioritized actionable findings and an implementation handoff listing assets, component mappings, interactions, tokens and open questions. For new designs, produce a concrete brief and use supported Figma creation tools only when connected and authorized; otherwise deliver a draft brief without claiming Figma edits.
