---
name: business-model
description: "Use when developing or revising a business model, business solution, product strategy or business requirements."
---

Read ../delivery-standards/references/shared-rules.md. Identify the customer and decision maker, problem, existing alternatives, value proposition, solution scope, channels, customer relationships, revenue assumptions, cost structure, partners, resources, activities, measurable outcomes and risks. Use source-backed evidence where available; label financial figures and demand claims as assumptions unless verified. Define an MVP and exclusions, domain entities, key workflows and constraints. Produce a business model canvas, a short solution brief and prioritized capabilities with hypothesis-validation plans. Explain how each capability contributes to an outcome. Use ../delivery-standards/references/templates.md and link accepted decisions into the knowledge index. Do not fabricate market research or treat a draft as an approved business plan.
